# Pairwise subjects

With a pairwise subject identifier, two clients in different sectors see two different `sub` for the same
person, and cannot correlate their users by comparing them. The sector is what the client declares, and the
identifier is stable for that sector.

This page will describe the calculation, the sector identifier, and what a client must do about it.
