# The account pages

A person reads their profile, their passkeys, their second factor, their open sessions, what they agreed to
and what every application holds of them, and revokes any of it without asking an administrator. The same
facts are available to an operator through the console, and the two read the same code.

This page will describe each page and what an action on it actually revokes.
