# Installing the bundle

Three things happen at installation: the bundle is registered, its routes are imported under the path of the
issuer, and its Doctrine migrations are run. [Getting started](../getting-started/installation.md) walks
through all three.

This page will describe the installation in detail, including what Flex does for you, what a deployment that
serves two planes imports, and how to check the result.
